What's inside

Standard + Scientific

Switch with one tap. Scientific functions live in a neat drawer, so keys never get tiny.

DEG & RAD

sin, cos, tan and their inverses follow the angle mode you choose.

Two themes

Midnight (dark) and Paper (light), each designed on its own.

Your colour

Six presets plus a slider to make any accent colour you like.

History

Every calculation is saved on your phone. Tap one to use it again.

Safe maths

Correct order of operations, brackets, and friendly errors instead of crashes.

Install in 1 minute

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download for Android.
  2. When it finishes, tap the file Lumen.apk.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps. You can switch it off again afterwards.
  4. Tap Install, then Open. Enjoy!
Google Play Protect shows a warning. Is that normal?

Yes. Lumen isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android shows a caution for any app installed from a website. Only install apps from sources you trust.

Does Lumen collect my data?

No. It asks for no permissions and has no internet features. History and settings stay on your phone.

What about iPhone?

Not yet. Apple apps can only be installed through the App Store, so that comes later.
